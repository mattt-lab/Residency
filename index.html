Your residency record, kept for you

Residency counts the days you spend in each US state or country, so you stay on the right side of 183-day rules. It stays on your iPhone: states, never addresses.

What it does

  • Counts days per state or country, with a clear total for the year.
  • Tracks in the background, so days are counted even when the app is not open. This needs location access set to Always.
  • Fills in your year from your photos or TripIt, so the months before you started are covered too.
  • Warns you before you cross a limit you set, such as 183 days.
  • Makes a year report for your tax advisor.
  • Stays on your iPhone. It keeps only the state and the time, never addresses or a map.

Private by design

Your location and travel data stay on your iPhone. The developer receives nothing. No accounts, no analytics, no ads. See the privacy policy.
